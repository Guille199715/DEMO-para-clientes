# Imagen de ejemplo

Herramienta: generacion de imagenes integrada.
Destino: hero.png, incorporada tambien en hero.js para funcionamiento sin conexion.

Use case: product-mockup. Create a polished photographic hero image for customizable boutique business website demos. ONE wide landscape bitmap, preferably 1536x1024. Light neutral white studio background, a quiet tabletop composition on the RIGHT HALF: a sculptural matte forest-green ceramic vase, a small beautifully bound white notebook and a clear glass vessel with one leafy branch. Real high-end still-life product photography, soft natural side light, tactile realistic surfaces, crisp visible objects, bright exposure, subtle grounded shadows. LEFT HALF is clean pale white negative space for website headlines, keep the entire left 40% almost empty and bright. Balanced refined contemporary shop aesthetic, no beige dominant palette, no gradients, no floating orbs. Objects belong on the tabletop, not floating. Camera frontal slightly above table height. Absolutely NO text, letters, logos, watermarks, people or screens. This is an illustrative default image; customers will replace it with their own product photography.
